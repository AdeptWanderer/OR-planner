Surgery Planning Lab

Weekly elective-surgery plans that account for ORs and the ICU / ward beds behind them, under uncertain surgery durations and lengths of stay. Compare a sample-average plan (SP) with two distributionally robust plans (DRO-M, DRO-W). Synthetic instances; precomputed with open-source solvers.

Method: Shehadeh, Tsang, Padman & Kilic, EJOR 329 (2026). Type parameters are reconstructed (hospital data is private); only ENT/OPHTH durations and VASCULAR stay are quoted in the paper. Out-of-sample metrics from 5,000 simulated weeks per plan.
